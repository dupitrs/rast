Privacy policy.

Updated 11 November 2026

Who handles your data?

The service provider under the rast brand is Raivis Reinfelds. Emins Nasirovs also helps review project enquiries. For privacy questions and requests, email hello@raststudio.lv.

Data and purposes

The form collects your name, email, selected service and message to answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal. Providing data is voluntary, but required fields are needed to submit the form; you can also email us. Please do not include identification numbers, passwords or sensitive data.

The basis for steps towards a contract at your request is GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Correspondence with business representatives and other enquiries relies on Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Legal and accounting obligations rely on Article 6(1)(c). Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. We do not profile visitors or make automated decisions.

The separate contact page also offers optional fields for phone, existing website, budget range, preferred start time and how you heard about us. We use these to assess and discuss your enquiry. The referral answer is supplied by you and is not browser tracking.

Recipients and international transfers

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative) receives and forwards form submissions and may process IP addresses and technical metadata. It operates from India and uses subprocessors outside the EEA. Its data processing agreement provides for EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request information about safeguards. See its privacy policy.
  • Google / Gmail handles email delivery, storage and replies. See its privacy policy and transfer safeguards.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the public website and may log IP addresses and request information. See its privacy statement.

Provider policies describe their processing regions and safeguards. We do not sell enquiry data.

Retention

We propose deleting enquiries that do not lead to a contract from our email within 12 months of the last contact. Contract records are retained for performance, statutory retention periods and, where necessary, legal claims.

Web3Forms states that it retains submissions for up to three years unless deleted earlier. This is separate from our email retention procedure. Provider logs and backups follow their respective retention rules.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email hello@raststudio.lv. We normally respond within one month and may request proportionate identity verification. You may complain to Latvia’s Data State Inspectorate.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies, Meta Pixel, visitor profiling or session recording. Browser history is used to restore your scroll position when you return.

This version therefore has no cookie consent banner. If consent-requiring technologies are introduced, we will provide options to accept, reject and withdraw consent before activating them. Browser cache can be cleared in browser settings.

External resources and links

Fonts load from Google Fonts; animation libraries load from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. These servers receive your IP address and technical request information when the page loads. The basis is our legitimate interest in displaying the website. Provider logs are covered by their respective policies: Google, Cloudflare, jsDelivr.

Social and portfolio links open external websites only when clicked. Those websites apply their own privacy policies.
