How to prepare for website development

A good website starts with clarity. The better you understand what you want to say and who you want to reach, the faster the project moves and the fewer misunderstandings arise. You do not need everything ready: a few essentials can make the process easier and less expensive.

Start with a goal

Decide what your website should achieve: attract clients, sell a product, accept bookings or create a trustworthy first impression. The goal shapes everything from the structure to the tone of the text. One clear goal keeps the website focused.

Content takes the most time

Content is a common cause of delays. A design may be ready, but the website cannot launch while text and images are still missing. It helps to prepare these early:

  • Text — who you are, what you offer and why someone should choose you.
  • Images — high-resolution photos of your products, team or premises.
  • Logo and brand — colours, fonts and existing brand materials.
  • Contact details — phone number, email and social media links.

If you do not have the text yet, we can work on it together.

Collect examples you like

A visual direction can be hard to explain in words. Send a few websites you like and explain what appeals to you: colours, layout or atmosphere. Examples you dislike are useful too, because they help define the boundaries.

The technical side

A few practical preparations make launch easier. If you already have a domain or website, arrange access. If you do not have a domain yet, we can help register one. It is also useful to gather:

  • Existing accounts — your Google profile, social media accounts and domain email addresses.
  • Old website content — text and images worth keeping.
  • Business details — company name, registration number and legal information.
