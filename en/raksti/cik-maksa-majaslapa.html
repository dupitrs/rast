How much does a website cost in Latvia?

The most common question has an honest first answer: “it depends”. That is because specific factors determine the cost of a website. Understanding them makes it much easier to compare proposals.

What determines the price

Most of the cost comes down to these factors:

  • Scope — the number of pages and complexity of features, such as forms, bookings, payments and multiple languages.
  • Design — a ready-made template or a custom design built around your brand.
  • Content — whether the text and images are ready or need to be prepared with the developer.
  • Integrations — payments, booking systems, a CRM and other tools that need to connect.
  • Deadline — a very short deadline usually increases the price.

Why prices vary so much

A simple business website, an online store and a custom business system can differ enormously in cost. A single price list would therefore be misleading. Templates are quicker and cheaper, but limit design and functionality. A custom solution costs more initially, but is built around your needs and can grow with your business.

What to compare in proposals

A price means little without context. Before deciding, check:

  • What is included: design, development, basic search optimisation, training and maintenance.
  • Who owns the result: the domain, code and content should belong to you.
  • How fast and usable the website will be on a phone: this affects visitors and search performance.
  • What happens after launch: support, updates and who to contact when something goes wrong.
