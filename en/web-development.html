Website development

A website is a salesperson that works for you around the clock. We build websites that don't just look good - they help you reach your goal: more calls, enquiries or sales.

What's included in a website project

We build every project from scratch, with no ready-made templates. The price always includes everything the website needs to actually work:

  • Custom design - a look created for your brand and your customers, not a repainted template.
  • Easy to use on a phone - most visitors come on their phones, so that's where we start, not where we finish.
  • Fast loading - a slow website loses both customers and Google rankings.
  • SEO essentials - a considered structure, headings and descriptions, so Google understands the site and shows it to the right people.
  • Contact form and analytics - so enquiries reach you and you can see what's happening on the site.

How a website gets built

From the first message to a finished website we go through four steps:

  1. Research. We get to know your business, goals and customers. That's how we understand what the website really has to achieve.
  2. Design. We set the style and work out every page of the site down to the smallest detail - and agree it all with you.
  3. Development. The design becomes a working website - fast and easy to use on a phone too.
  4. Launch. We publish the site, check that everything works, and stick around with support.

A simpler website can be ready in a few days, a bigger project in a few weeks. We agree on a clear timeline right at the start.

How much does a website cost

The simplest websites start from €400. Beyond that, the price depends on scope and features - how many pages you need, and whether you want forms, bookings, payments or multiple languages. After a short conversation we prepare a clear, no-obligation quote with no hidden costs.
