The challengeRecord keeping that took too much time

The association “Mums pieder pasaule” involves young people in volunteering, but keeping records was the hardest part. Hours worked, completed tasks and individual contributions were recorded by hand, in spreadsheets or even in WhatsApp messages.

This took time and introduced errors and a risk of losing data. Young people also lacked a clear way to see their progress and receive recognition for their work.

The solutionOne system for records and motivation

We built a central system for volunteer records and motivation. Volunteers register, apply for events and tasks, record their hours and follow their progress. The organisation administrator reviews and approves the records and generates PDF certificates with one click.

  • Opportunities and applications with status tracking
  • Recorded hours with administrator approval
  • PDF certificates for volunteer hours
  • Motivation through points, levels, missions and rewards
  • Profiles with map address search and secure sign-in

The resultLess manual work, more motivation

The system reduces manual work, speeds up certificate preparation and keeps records in a clear, secure database. The motivation features help young people feel involved and appreciated. The solution can also be adapted for other youth organisations.
